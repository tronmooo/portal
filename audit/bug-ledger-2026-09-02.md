### Not a bug / by design (noted)
- Paying the same bill twice within 8 s is folded into one payment (D29's rule); the probe waits 9 s between real payments.
- `DELETE /api/expenses|tasks|incomes/:id` are deliberately idempotent and answer 200 for an id that does not exist for this user (nothing is deleted, nothing leaks); the other entity deletes answer 404.
- `POST /api/journal` for a day that already has an entry appends to it (one entry per day, `journal_entries_unique_day`) and answers 200 with `appended: true`.
- Upcoming bills is a 30-day window: a bill whose next due date moved past it (after a skip + a payment) is correctly absent.
- `POST /api/goals` answers 200 rather than 201 (inconsistent with the other creates; harmless).
- ⌘K search under the Self scope returns nothing for "Linda": the server scopes `/api/search` by the active filter (documented in CommandSearch.tsx). By design, though a person's own profile card arguably should always be findable — noted, not changed.
- The client calendar month grid uses the strict `out_of_scope` orphan policy while the server timeline uses `belongs_to_self` (an unlinked event shows on the Self timeline API but the grid comment says unowned items must never leak into a person's calendar). Deliberate per its comment; noted.
- `GET /api/profiles/:id/ai-summary` answers 500 on the replica because no `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is set; the page degrades to "Summary unavailable". Environment, not code.
- The Habits page (and every list) defaults to the Self scope (profileFilter v5), so a habit linked to the pet is hidden until Biscuit or Everyone is selected; the header says "· Local-tester" to show the scope. By design.
- STREAK on the hub strip is the best of habit streaks and the journal streak ("2D" with a 2-day journal streak and no habit streak). By design, if terse.
- Two live "Mow the lawn" tasks are the done 2026-08-26 occurrence and its spawned 2026-09-02 clone (recurring spawn-on-complete). By design.
- Paying a recurring bill logs a matching expense (`autoLogExpense` defaults on), so MTD spend rises by the bill amount; `stats.monthlySpend` and the snapshot agree.
- Deleting a profile (person, pet, car, account…) is an atomic hard cascade in Postgres (`delete_profile_cascade`) behind a confirm dialog; there is no restore route and its children (the car's insurance bill) go with it while linked expenses are soft-deleted. Confirmed destructive by design; noted, not changed. The seed's Honda Civic and Car insurance were recreated after the probe.
- Plain REST creates for expenses, events and tasks have no server-side double-submit dedupe (probe s14: two identical POSTs in the same tick → two rows). The dialogs disable Save while the mutation is pending, mutations never retry, and there is no offline replay, so a duplicate needs two independent submits; the chat paths dedupe on their own. Left as is — a server rule would also swallow a deliberate second identical entry. Noted.
- Two simultaneous completes of a recurring task spawn exactly one next occurrence; two simultaneous check-ins on a once-a-day habit leave one check-in (s14).
- Notification `type` for a task due in 1–2 days is `task_due_today` (title says "due in 2 days"); the bell treats overdue/today/soon identically (icon + deep link), so nothing is misrendered. Cosmetic; noted.
- SAVINGS RATE ("income − spend") ignores recurring bills while CASH FLOW on the same screen subtracts them (95% vs 90% on the seed). The tile says what it computes; a definitional choice, noted.
- `PATCH /api/goals/:id {unit: ""}` is accepted (`unit: z.string()`); a custom goal without a unit is legitimate ("10 ×"), so left as is.
- Journal `mood` is a word enum (amazing … terrible), not a 1–5 number; the probe's numeric mood was rejected with a clear 400 and the stats mood followed the last entry. Not a bug.
- `PATCH /api/obligations/:id {amount: 0}` is accepted: `insertObligationSchema.amount` is deliberately non-negative (a variable or usage-based bill can have a $0 base, and a free trial is a $0 subscription), the create route accepts the same, and a $0 bill contributes $0 everywhere. Probe s8 flagged it; not a bug.
