## What you'll learn
- What counts as a liability in Portol: loans, mortgages, credit cards and recurring bills
- Three ways to add one by hand, plus how to add one by just telling the chat
- Everything you can do on a liability's page: payments, payoff planning, links, documents, history
- Where your debts show up across the app: net worth, the Finance page, your calendar

## 3. Add a car loan by hand
1. Click **+**. ![](screenshots/desktop/L2.01-a.png)
2. Pick **Auto Loan**. ![](screenshots/desktop/L2.03-a.png) ![](screenshots/desktop/L2.03-b.png)
3. Click **Next**. ![](screenshots/desktop/L2.04-a.png)
4. Fill in the details. Fields marked * are required. If you skip one, Portol points it out:
   ![](screenshots/desktop/L2.05-b.png)
   ![](screenshots/desktop/L2.06-f.png) ![](screenshots/desktop/L2.06-f2.png)
5. Click **Create**. ![](screenshots/desktop/L2.07-a.png) ![](screenshots/desktop/L2.07-b.png)
6. Your loan now appears in the list. ![](screenshots/desktop/L2.08-b.png)

> **Tip:** Portol warns you if a liability with the same name already exists. ![](screenshots/desktop/L2.09-b.png)
